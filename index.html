About Kotla Medical Store

Kotla Medical Store is a trusted healthcare store serving the local community in Kovvur, Andhra Pradesh.

We provide medicines, healthcare essentials, first-aid products, personal care items and other everyday medical needs.

Our aim is to make essential healthcare products easily accessible while providing friendly and responsible customer service.

We believe in treating every customer with care, respect and attention.
